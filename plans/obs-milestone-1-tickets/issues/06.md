## What to build

A client lists scenes for a supplied canvas UUID without silently falling back to another canvas.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Carry canvasUuid from the listed public schema through request dispatch under the recorded official contract; preserve existing unscoped resource behavior and document locator applicability.
- [ ] Use installed MCP with multiple canvases and duplicate scene names to verify outgoing identity and returned scope independently.
- [ ] Preserve unscoped/name-compatible behavior and capability/toolset policy; invalid or unsupported identity fails explicitly.
- [ ] Update public documentation and retire the corresponding parity finding with passing field and behavior evidence.
