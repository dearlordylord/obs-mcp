[Prepare and gate publication of the exact OBS package that passes acceptance](https://github.com/dearlordylord/obs-mcp/issues/12)

## What to build

Release preparation produces one attributable candidate whose tested bytes are the bytes offered for publication.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Build/pack once for the candidate and install/test that exact artifact on the supported runtime matrix; retain its identity, version, source/lock provenance and digest.
- [ ] Make the existing publication path consume the verified tarball rather than rebuild or publish the source directory.
- [ ] Require acceptance evidence to match the candidate and fail on missing, wrong or changed artifacts; rehearse at a controlled publication boundary without publishing.
- [ ] Account for every supported release entry point, including local release, so documented alternate paths cannot bypass the same candidate checks.
- [ ] Keep existing authenticated publication policy and separate release preparation from maintainer authorization.
