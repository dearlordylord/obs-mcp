## What to build

An explicitly authorized release is attributable to the accepted candidate and verified from the registry.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Obtain maintainer authorization for the identified candidate before any actual npm publication; ticket creation and plan approval are not that authorization.
- [ ] Confirm quality, supported-runtime acceptance and real OBS/client evidence all identify the same unchanged candidate.
- [ ] Publish through the prepared path and verify exact registry identity/integrity; record release evidence and any retry outcome.
- [ ] Install the registry artifact into a fresh consumer and verify its identity and semantic controlled-OBS acceptance.
- [ ] Leave release completion unresolved on conflict, missing evidence or unavailable authorization; preserve later milestone and investigation continuation.
